Thresholds & scoring

Search uses your Target roles above as the niches (plus a few skills). Add roles → they get searched.

Each lead scores 0–100. These are the points added when a signal is present, then the total is scaled by confidence:

What do these mean?
  • Hiring intent — the post is a real hiring request (not a job seeker). Every lead gets this.
  • Role match — its job title matches one of your Target roles.
  • Skill match — it mentions one of your Target skills.
  • Budget — it states a budget or rate ($20k, $50/hr).
  • Company — a company name was identified.
  • Recent — the post is recent (within the recency window).

The total is then scaled by classifier confidence. Priority: HIGH ≥ 80, MEDIUM ≥ 50, LOW below.
